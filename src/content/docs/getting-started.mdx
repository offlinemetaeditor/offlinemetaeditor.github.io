Offline Meta Editor is a Windows program for Meta ad accounts. You download an account, edit it on your
computer, ask Meta to check the changes and then upload them. This page takes you from nothing to your first
change.

:::caution[Beta]
The editor is new. Before every upload, read the list the Push window shows you, and try your first upload
on something that is paused.
:::

## What you need

- **Windows 10 or 11**, 64-bit.
- **A Meta access token** that can reach your ad accounts, with the `ads_read` permission to download and
  `ads_management` to upload. [Access token](/access-token/) explains how to get one.
- **Internet** to download, check and upload. Editing, browsing and filtering work offline.

## Install

<Download />

There are two programs, and neither needs an installer:

- `mae-gui.exe` is the editor, with its window. This is the one most people want.
- `mae.exe` is the [command line](/reference/command-line/): the same operations, for scripts.

Put them in a folder of your choice, for example `C:\Programs\Offline Meta Editor`, and double-click
`mae-gui.exe`. To update, replace the files with newer ones: your accounts and settings live elsewhere
(see [Settings and data folder](/reference/settings-and-data/)).

:::note[Windows protected your PC]
The programs are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may stop the first start. Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.
:::

## First run

<Steps>

1. **Give the editor your token.** Click **⚙** at the right end of the toolbar (or press
   <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>). Under *Use a different access token*, paste the token and click
   **Use for this session**, or **Save** to keep it. Then click **Test token**: the editor shows whose token
   it is, its permissions and when it expires. [Access token](/access-token/) has the details and the other
   ways to provide it.

2. **Download an account.** Click **📂 Accounts** (or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>). Under
   *Ad accounts on Meta*, click **Load list**, find the account and click **Download**. Big accounts take
   a little while the first time (about 20 seconds for 2,000 ads); later downloads are much faster.

   Options before you download: **Include archived** also brings archived campaigns, ad sets and ads, and
   **Only campaign ids** downloads just the campaigns you list.

3. **Look around.** The account opens in a tab. Campaigns and ad sets are in the tree on the left, the
   table in the middle shows one level (Campaigns, Ad sets or Ads) and Properties, on the right, shows the
   row you clicked. [The editor window](/guides/editor-window/) explains each part.

4. **Make a change.** Click an ad set and change its name in Properties. The row gets an amber **●**
   (changed) and the toolbar button now reads **⬆ Push 1 change**. Your change is saved on your computer
   by itself.

5. **Check it.** Click **✔ Check** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>). Meta runs its validations and applies
   nothing; the result shows in the **Results** tab at the bottom.

6. **Send it.** Click **⬆ Push 1 change** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>). The window lists every
   operation, with old and new values. Click **⬆ Push 1 change(s) now** to send it, or **Cancel**.

</Steps>

Changed your mind before pushing? Select the row and click **↺ Revert**, or press
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> to undo the last edit.
