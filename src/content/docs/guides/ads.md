As in Ads Manager, the creative is part of the ad: there is no separate Creatives level. Click an ad and its
Properties show, from top to bottom, a preview, who the ad runs as, its fields and its **Ad creative**.

## Editing an ad changes only that ad

Meta does not let anyone edit a creative once it exists. So when you change an ad's text, link, media or
identity, the editor gives that ad its own copy of the creative, created on push. That has two consequences:

- **Only that ad changes**, even when several ads share the creative.
- **The ad goes through Meta's review again** after the push, as it would if you edited it in Ads Manager.

Put the content back the way it was and the ad uses its original creative again, so nothing is sent.
Reverting or deleting the ad drops its copy. The toolbar counts an edited ad as one change.

## Texts and links

The **Ad creative** section holds the primary text, headline, description, website URL, call to action and
**URL parameters** (the UTM tags added to the link). Flexible creatives have several variations of each
text: add, change or remove lines.

Ads that use an existing post, and creatives where Meta picks among several media on its own (dynamic
creative), are shown but not changed here.

## Images and videos per placement

**Media** has one line per group of placements, the way Ads Manager builds them: for example *Stories &
Reels* (9:16) and *Everything else* (feeds and the rest). Point at a line to see its placements.

- **Change ▾** or **Choose ▾** opens a picker with the images and videos the account's ads already use
  (offline, with thumbnails and sizes). **Load the account's library from Meta** adds its newest 100 images
  and 100 videos. Filter by name, images or videos.
- An ad with one image or video everywhere shows *All placements* and **Different media for Stories &
  Reels**, which splits it into two lines.

:::note
Uploading new image or video files from your computer is not supported yet. Upload them in Ads Manager
first; then pick them here.
:::

## Facebook page and Instagram account

Under the ad's name, **Facebook page ▾** and **Instagram ▾** show who the ad runs as, with their pictures:

- **Facebook page ▾** lists the pages the ad account can advertise as. Picking one also sets that page's
  Instagram account.
- **Instagram ▾** offers the page's account, the accounts other ads of the account use, and **Use the
  Facebook page**.

**↺** puts back what the ad has on Meta. Ads that use an existing post keep the page and account they were
posted with.

## New ads and copied content

**➕ New ad** creates an empty ad in the selected ad set: texts, link and media to fill in, with the two media
lines (*Stories & Reels* and *Everything else*). It runs as the ad set's other ads do (page, Instagram
account, URL parameters and tracking).

**Use the content of another ad** gives the ad the same texts, links and media as another ad. It is the
quickest way to fill a new ad.

## Previews

- The **preview card** in Properties shows the ad the way people see it: page or Instagram account, primary
  text, image or video, link, headline and call to action. **Facebook | Instagram** at its top switches
  between the two. Flexible creatives show every image or video as thumbnails.
- **Meta's preview ▾** opens Meta's own rendering in your browser: Facebook feed, Instagram feed, Stories or
  Reels. An ad you edited is previewed from your local version, so you can see new copy before you push.
- The **Preview** column of the Ads table shows a thumbnail of each ad. Hide it in **⚙ Columns**.

Images are kept on your computer after the first time they show, so previews work offline.
