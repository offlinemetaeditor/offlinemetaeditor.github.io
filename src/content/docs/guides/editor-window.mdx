1. **Menu.** Every command, with its keyboard shortcut at the right.
2. **Tabs.** Each tab shows one account with its own view. See [Tabs and favorites](/guides/tabs-and-favorites/).
3. **Toolbar.** The account's name, id, currency, time zone and when it was downloaded; **API 12%** is how
   much of Meta's limit the account has used (see [How syncing works](/reference/how-sync-works/#api-limits)).
   At the right: **📂 Accounts**, **Audiences**, **Events Manager**, **⟳ Download**, **✔ Check**,
   **⬆ Push N changes** and **⚙** Settings. "unsaved" appears for a moment after an edit, until the editor
   saves it.
4. **Favorites bar.** Saved selections and views. Tools → Favorites bar hides it.
5. **Tree.** The campaigns and their ad sets, with a status dot. Click a campaign to see its ad sets, or an ad
   set to see its ads; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click adds or removes one. The box at the top searches, and the two
   lists below order the tree (Meta's order, name, active first, newest, oldest, biggest budget, pending
   changes first) and filter it.
6. **Levels.** **Campaigns**, **Ad sets** and **Ads**, with the number of rows and a search by name or id.
7. **Actions.** **➕ New** and, while rows are selected, what you can do with them: **▶ Activate** or
   **⏸ Pause**, **Budget ▾**, **Duplicate ▾**, **Rename ▾**, **↺ Revert**, **More ▾** and **🗑 Delete**.
   At the right, **View ▾** and **⚙ Columns**.
8. **Table.** One row per campaign, ad set or ad.
9. **Properties.** Everything about the row you clicked, ready to edit.
10. **Log and Results.** What the editor did, and the outcome of the last check or push.

## Selecting rows

Click a row to select it, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click to add or remove rows, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click to select a
range. With the keyboard, the arrows move between rows and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+arrows select as they go.

**What you select limits the levels below, as in Ads Manager.** Select two campaigns and the next tabs read
*Ad sets for 2 campaigns* and *Ads for 2 campaigns*, and show only theirs. Each level keeps its own
selection; **2 selected ✖** next to a level clears it. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd> goes down a level into
the selected rows, and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> comes back up.

## What the marks mean

The first column of the table shows what will happen on the next push:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **+** (green) | New: created on push |
| **●** (amber) | Changed: point at it to see which fields |
| **✖** (red) | Deleted on push |
| **!** (red) | Meta refused the last attempt: point at it, or see *Last error* in Properties |

Changed values are amber in the table, and in Properties the field's name is amber with a **↺** to put back
Meta's value. Point at the name to see the value on Meta.

**Status and delivery** have a colored dot before Meta's words: green is running; gray is off (paused,
campaign paused, archived); blue waits on Meta (processing, in review); amber needs attention (with issues,
billing); red is rejected or deleted.

## Columns, sorting and filters

- **⚙ Columns** shows, hides and orders the columns of each level. Besides every field there are columns
  that the editor works out: budget and where it is set, conversion event, pixel, locations, age, gender,
  placements, custom audiences, attribution, number of ad sets or ads and, for ads, their texts, link, call
  to action, URL parameters, format, media, page and Instagram account, pending changes and last error.
- **Sort** by clicking a column title: ascending, descending, then back to Meta's order.
- **Filter** with **View ▾ → Filter boxes under the column titles**, then type under a title:

  | Type | Shows rows where the column |
  |---|---|
  | `text` | contains *text* |
  | `!text` | does not contain *text* |
  | `=text` | is exactly *text* |
  | `>50` `>=50` `<10` `<=10` | is a number above or below (budgets in currency units) |
  | `empty` / `!empty` | is empty / is not empty |
  | `a\|b` | contains *a* or *b* |

- **View ▾ → Show** keeps only active, not active, in review or with issues, with pending changes, or with
  errors. **Hide archived/deleted** hides those.

While anything hides rows, the count turns red: **Showing 6 of 11 rows ✖**. Point at it to see why, and click
it (or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>L</kbd>) to show every row again.

Columns, sorting and Show are remembered the next time you open the editor.

## Themes

**⚙ Settings → Appearance** picks the theme: follow Windows, Light, Dark or **Frutiger Aero**, a Windows 7
look with glass panels over a sky-blue background. You can also use the Windows font (Segoe UI) and change
the zoom.
