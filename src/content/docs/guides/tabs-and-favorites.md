## Tabs

Each tab shows one account with its own view: the level, what is selected at each level, the search, Show,
column filters and sorting. Several tabs can show the same account (an edit in one shows in the others) or
different accounts. A **•** marks tabs whose account has pending changes.

- **➕** opens a new tab: the same account, a copy of this tab, another downloaded account, or another account
  to download.
- In the **Accounts** window, **Open in a new tab** decides whether an account opens in a new tab or in the
  current one.
- Right-click a tab to rename, duplicate, move or close it, or to close the others. Middle-click it, or click
  its **×**, to close it.
- Keys: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> (or
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Page Down</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Page Up</kbd>) go to the next and previous
  tab, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>8</kbd> to a tab, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>9</kbd> to the
  last one, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd> closes the tab.

Tabs come back when you open the editor again. Only the active tab's account loads at start; the others load
when you switch to them.

## Favorites

The favorites bar sits under the toolbar (**Tools → Favorites bar** shows or hides it). A favorite is one of
two things:

- **⭐ Selected items**: the campaigns, ad sets or ads you selected. Opening it shows only those and what is
  inside them, with a banner that says so; **✖ show all** lifts the limit. Under a favorite with a single
  campaign, **➕ New ad set** goes into that campaign.
- **🔖 A view**: the level, the campaign or ad set, Show, the search, column filters and sorting.

Click **⭐ Add to favorites** to pick one, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd>: it saves the selection, or
the view when nothing is selected.

Click a favorite to open it in the current tab (switching account when needed); <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click or
middle-click opens it in a new tab. Right-click it to open it in a new tab, replace it with the current
selection or filters, rename, move or delete it. Favorites of other accounts show the account's name.
