## Why an offline editor

Ads Manager is built for one change at a time. Offline Meta Editor downloads the whole account to your
computer, lets you change hundreds of things in minutes, and sends them to Meta in one go, after Meta has
checked them.

## How it works

1. **Download** an ad account. Your token reads it from Meta's Marketing API.
2. **Edit** on your computer. Every change is kept on your computer and shows as pending until you send it.
3. **Check**: Meta runs all of its validations on your changes and applies nothing.
4. **Push** the changes. You see the full list first, and nothing is sent until you confirm.

Downloading again later keeps your pending edits: the editor merges what changed on Meta with what you
changed here.

## What you can edit

- **Campaigns**: name, status, budget, bid strategy, spend cap, schedule.
- **Ad sets**: budget and schedule, the conversion (pixel and event), targeting (places, age, gender,
  languages, custom audiences, detailed targeting, placements, devices), attribution on new ad sets,
  frequency cap.
- **Ads**: texts, website, call to action, URL parameters, images and videos per placement, the Facebook
  page and Instagram account the ad runs as, with a preview.
- **Library**: pixels and their recent events, custom conversions and audiences (custom, website, lookalike
  and customer lists hashed on your computer).
