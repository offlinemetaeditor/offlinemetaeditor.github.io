---
title: Data deletion
description: How to delete everything Offline Meta Editor keeps and disconnect Offline Ads Editor from Facebook.
---

Offline Meta Editor keeps your data only on your computer, and our login server keeps nothing, so you can
delete everything yourself:

1. **Delete the access token.** In the editor, open **Settings** (⚙ at the right end of the toolbar) and, in
   **Meta account and access token**, click **Delete the stored token…**.
2. **Delete the downloaded data.** Close the editor and delete the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\meta-ads-editor`
   (paste the path in the File Explorer address bar to open it).
3. **Disconnect the app from Facebook.** Open your
   [Facebook business integrations](https://www.facebook.com/settings/?tab=business_tools) (Settings &
   privacy, Settings, Business integrations), select **Offline Ads Editor** and click **Remove**. Meta then
   stops accepting the token the editor had.

That's all: no copy of your Meta data exists anywhere else. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) for
details.

to [lucas@gorgal.com](mailto:lucas@gorgal.com) with the subject "Data deletion".
